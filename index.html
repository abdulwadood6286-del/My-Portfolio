CASE STUDY 02
MEDICAL WELLNESS • CRM • MEMBERSHIP AUTOMATION

Noor Esthétique & Wellness

Built a complete patient lead, appointment, membership, communication, and reputation management system using GoHighLevel and Pabau.

medical_services

NOOR ESTHÉTIQUE & WELLNESS Home Page

Problem

Noor Esthétique & Wellness needed one connected system to manage website enquiries, service-specific forms, appointment booking, lead follow-up, membership activity, communication preferences, CRM pipelines, and reputation requests. Customer and membership information was divided between GoHighLevel and Pabau, making it difficult to keep contact records and membership statuses consistently updated.

Solution

Built a complete GoHighLevel CRM and automation system connected with Pabau. Every Noor service uses its own form and automation path, with welcome emails, appointment confirmation, no-booking nurture, membership link delivery after attendance, Pabau webhooks syncing membership activity, a communication-preference workflow, and a full CRM pipeline that triggers a review request once a contact reaches the Member stage.

Result

Noor now has one connected customer journey covering lead capture, service selection, booking, follow-up, appointment attendance, membership conversion, membership status updates, communication preferences, pipeline management, and review requests. The system reduces manual updates and keeps appointment and membership data organized inside GoHighLevel.

AGENCY OPERATIONS • CRM • CLIENT ONBOARDING

VEGA MIDEIA CO.

ACTIVE INTEGRATION
account_tree

VEGA MIDEIA CO. CRM & Client Delivery Pipeline

Problem

VEGA MIDEIA CO. needed one connected system to manage the complete client journey from website enquiry to appointment booking, sales consultation, contract signing, invoicing, onboarding, project delivery, monthly reporting, and review collection — while using GoHighLevel, ClickUp, Google Drive, Google Sheets, Zapier, AgencyAnalytics, BrightLocal, and LandingSite.

Solution

Built a complete end-to-end agency operations system using GoHighLevel as the main CRM, with a welcome email and booking link, ClickUp tasks and Google Sheets records created at booking, contracts and invoices triggered after a Showed appointment, an Onboarding pipeline stage after payment, a launch-planning call after onboarding, an Active Client stage with a review request after delivery, a Zapier-driven Google Drive client folder on contact creation, and monthly reporting through Zapier, AgencyAnalytics, and BrightLocal.

Result

VEGA MIDEIA CO. now has one connected client journey from first enquiry to active-client management. Sales, appointments, ClickUp tasks, Google Sheets records, contracts, invoices, onboarding, Google Drive folders, reporting, pipeline stages, and review requests are coordinated through one structured system.

CASE STUDY 04
WEBINAR FUNNEL • GOHIGHLEVEL AUTOMATION

Webinar Registration

Built an automated webinar registration and post-event booking system that routes attendees into the correct consultation calendar.

video_library

Webinar Registration

Problem

Webinar leads needed a structured journey from registration to attendance follow-up and consultation booking. Without automation, it was difficult to consistently welcome registrants, identify attendees, collect their service preference, and direct them to the correct booking calendar.

Solution

Built a complete GoHighLevel webinar automation system. The system:

  • Captures webinar registrations
  • Sends an immediate welcome email
  • Checks whether the contact attended the webinar
  • Sends attendees a consultation-selection form
  • Allows the attendee to choose between a Strategy Call and a Funding Call
  • Triggers the correct booking workflow based on the selected service
  • Sends the relevant calendar link and booking communication

Result

Each webinar contact follows the correct automated path based on attendance and service selection. Attendees receive a clear next step, while Strategy Call and Funding Call leads are automatically separated and directed into the appropriate booking journey.

FAQ

Questions you might have.

Do you work only with GoHighLevel? add

GoHighLevel is my main platform and where most of my build experience lives, but I also work with Zapier, Make, and direct API/webhook integrations when a project calls for connecting outside tools.

Can you improve an existing CRM setup? add

Yes. I regularly audit existing GoHighLevel accounts, fix broken or incomplete automations, and rebuild pipelines and workflows that have grown messy over time — without starting from scratch unless that's genuinely the faster path.

How long does a CRM implementation take? add

It depends on scope. A focused build (one funnel plus its automations) can take about a week; a full CRM system with multiple pipelines, integrations, and AI agents usually runs 3–5 weeks. You'll get a specific timeline after the discovery call.

Do you provide ongoing support? add

Every build includes a support window after launch to catch anything real usage turns up. Beyond that, several clients keep me on for ongoing maintenance and iteration — happy to set that up if it's useful for you.

Can you build custom automations? add

Yes — that's most of what I do. Multi-step workflows, conditional branching, webhook-triggered actions, cross-platform syncing with tools like ClickUp or Google Sheets, all built around how your business actually operates.

Do you set up AI Voice Agents? add

Yes. I've built and deployed live AI voice and chat agents that run real conversations, qualify leads, and write answers straight into GoHighLevel contact fields — not a demo, actively running on real contacts.
