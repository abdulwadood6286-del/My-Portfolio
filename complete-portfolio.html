CASE STUDY 01
AUTOMOTIVE DETAILING • GOHIGHLEVEL CRM & AUTOMATION

Element 6 Auto SPA

Built a complete lead-to-customer automation system covering enquiries, appointment booking, invoicing, payments, follow-up, pipelines, and after-hours communication.

report_problemProblem

Element 6 Auto SPA needed a connected system to capture website enquiries, respond to new leads, encourage appointment booking, manage pipeline movement, send invoices, track payments, and follow up with contacts who did not book. The business also needed an automated response for customers who sent messages outside normal business hours.

lightbulbSolution

Built a complete GoHighLevel customer journey beginning from the website contact form. The system includes:

  • Website lead capture
  • Welcome email with appointment booking link
  • Appointment booking confirmation
  • CRM pipeline management
  • Invoice delivery
  • Invoice payment tracking
  • No-response nurture follow-up
  • After-business-hours messaging workflow

check_circleResult

Every new enquiry now enters one connected system. Leads receive an immediate response, are guided toward booking, move through the CRM pipeline, receive invoices, and continue through the correct follow-up path based on their actions. The business can manage the full customer journey with less manual work and more consistent communication.

CASE STUDY 02
WEBINAR FUNNEL • GOHIGHLEVEL AUTOMATION

Webinar Registration

Built an automated webinar registration and post-event booking system that routes attendees into the correct consultation calendar.

video_library

Webinar Registration

Problem

Webinar leads needed a structured journey from registration to attendance follow-up and consultation booking. Without automation, it was difficult to consistently welcome registrants, identify attendees, collect their service preference, and direct them to the correct booking calendar.

Solution

Built a complete GoHighLevel webinar automation system. The system:

  • Captures webinar registrations
  • Sends an immediate welcome email
  • Checks whether the contact attended the webinar
  • Sends attendees a consultation-selection form
  • Allows the attendee to choose between a Strategy Call and a Funding Call
  • Triggers the correct booking workflow based on the selected service
  • Sends the relevant calendar link and booking communication

Result

Each webinar contact follows the correct automated path based on attendance and service selection. Attendees receive a clear next step, while Strategy Call and Funding Call leads are automatically separated and directed into the appropriate booking journey.
